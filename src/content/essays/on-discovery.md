---
title: On discovery
author: Sangwoong Yoon
date: '2026-11-11'
---

All research is fundamentally about discovery, and discovery is not necessarily rational. Rationality operates within a framework built on what we already know, but our knowledge captures only a tiny fraction of how the world actually works. It is only natural that chance and luck play major roles in discovery, as with penicillin and X-rays. New discoveries often seem unbelievable or defy existing theories. But when our predictions, based on limited knowledge, conflict with what we observe in nature, which should we trust? This is why we must approach nature with humility, curiosity, and an open mind. Because we work with computers, we often forget that we, too, are studying nature. Algorithms and everything that happens inside computers obey the laws of nature. We are simply learning more about nature, little by little.

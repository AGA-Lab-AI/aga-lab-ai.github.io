## Research Internship

### Goal and Scope

- The goal of the project is to publish a paper at a top-tier AI conference.
- Each intern will be given a clear project description and may collaborate with other researchers.
- An intern may join an ongoing project.

### Logistics

- The internship lasts from three to six months, with the exact duration potentially varying.
- The internship is full-time and on-site at UNIST in Ulsan, Republic of Korea. A salary will be provided.

### Selection

- A background in machine learning is required and will be evaluated through a verbal examination.
